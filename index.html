The Island

A resort shaped by tide, not blueprint.

Coral Cloud Resorts sits on a single crescent-shaped atoll where a living reef meets deep lagoon water. Every residence was sited before it was designed — the overwater villas float where the reef drops off into blue, the beach bungalows sit where the sand is softest, and the estates claim the quiet edges where the island runs out.

There is no single architectural style here. Instead, fifteen distinct residences, each responding to its own patch of the island — timber and thatch on the shore, glass and bleached teak over the water, stone and palm in the hillside canopy.
