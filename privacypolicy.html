1. Overview

Coral Cloud Resorts ("we," "us," "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us — whether you're browsing our Site, requesting a quote through our AI concierge, or staying with us as a guest. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it's used, and the choices available to you.

By using coralcloudresorts.com (the "Site") or providing information to us in connection with a reservation, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly

  • Reservation details: name, email address, phone number, mailing address, arrival/departure dates, party size, and special requests.
  • Payment information: credit card or payment details, processed through our PCI-DSS-compliant payment provider. We do not store full card numbers on our own servers.
  • Communications: messages sent via our contact forms, email, telephone, or the Coral Cloud Agent chat concierge.
  • Account information: if you create a guest profile, your login credentials and stored preferences (e.g. room type, dietary needs).

Information collected automatically

  • Device & usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, referring URL, and approximate location (derived from IP), collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Analytics data: aggregated, pseudonymized behavioral data used to understand how visitors use the Site.

3. The Coral Cloud Agent Chat Concierge

Our Site includes an AI-assisted chat concierge, the Coral Cloud Agent, built on Salesforce Agentforce / Embedded Messaging technology. When you use this chat feature:

  • The content of your conversation, along with basic technical information (browser, approximate location, session identifiers), is transmitted to and processed by Salesforce, Inc. as our service provider, in order to generate responses and maintain conversation continuity as you move between pages on our Site.
  • If you provide personal information in the chat (such as your name, email, or travel dates) to receive a recommendation or quote, that information is handled under this Privacy Policy and may be used to follow up with you regarding your inquiry.
  • Conversation transcripts may be reviewed by our reservations team to improve response quality and resolve guest inquiries, and may be retained for a limited period as described in Section 7.
  • Please avoid sharing sensitive information (such as full payment card numbers or government ID numbers) directly in the chat window; our reservations team will always request this through a secure channel when needed.
The Coral Cloud Agent's responses are generated automatically and are provided for convenience. See our Terms & Conditions for details on how chat interactions relate to confirmed bookings.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and manage your reservation, including confirmations, changes, and cancellations;
  • To communicate with you about your stay, respond to inquiries, and provide customer support;
  • To personalize your experience, including remembering preferences for future stays;
  • To process payments and prevent fraud;
  • To send marketing communications, where you have opted in (you may unsubscribe at any time);
  • To analyze and improve the Site, our services, and the performance of the Coral Cloud Agent; and
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax, safety, and immigration record-keeping requirements applicable to hospitality providers.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only in the following circumstances:

6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember your preferences, understand usage patterns, and — where you've consented — support marketing efforts. This includes:

  • Essential cookies: required for core Site functionality, such as maintaining your chat session as you move between pages.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors interact with the Site.
  • Marketing cookies: used, with consent, to tailor promotional content.

You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling essential cookies may affect Site functionality, including the chat concierge's ability to remember your conversation across pages.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, including:

  • Reservation and guest stay records: retained for 7 years to meet tax and financial record-keeping obligations;
  • Chat concierge transcripts: retained for up to 24 months to support quality review and dispute resolution, unless a shorter period is requested and legally permitted;
  • Marketing preferences: retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion.

8. Data Security

We use industry-standard technical and organizational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and PCI-DSS-compliant payment handling — to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate information;
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal retention requirements;
  • Object to or restrict certain processing, including marketing;
  • Request a portable copy of your data; and
  • Withdraw consent at any time where processing is based on consent.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

10. International Data Transfers

Coral Cloud Resorts and our service providers, including Salesforce, may process and store information in countries other than your own. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards — such as standard contractual clauses — to ensure your information receives an adequate level of protection wherever it is processed.

11. Children's Privacy

The Site and our reservation services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
